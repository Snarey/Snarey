FJS 200I’m Finbarre, an arts and culture journalist, producer and creative technologist based in Beeston, Nottinghamshire.

I build world-first interview formats around tarot, chance and creative prompts.

Featured guests across my projects include Joanne Harris, Lauren Beukes, Adrian Tchaikovsky, Julian Simpson, Lucy Rose, C.B. Lee and Sacha Coward.

My journey through disability, reinvention and building creative projects was profiled in The Daily Mirror.

Available for comment, interviews, podcast bookings, festival coverage, research enquiries and collaborations.

Tarot Interviews

A podcast where writers, artists and cultural figures discuss their work through tarot cards as structured prompts.

Season One is available through major podcast platforms. Future episodes will be released as standalone specials, beginning with festival and event coverage.

Tarot DMs

A messenger-based interview series where creative guests respond to three tarot cards through text, images, voice notes and short clips.

The format is designed to be accessible and low-pressure, creating a highly shareable digital scrapbook of creative lives.

British Tarot Archive

An independent archive documenting tarot decks, printed material and contemporary Rider-Waite-Smith practice connected with Britain and the UK.

The archive supports research into tarot as culture, design, publishing history and living heritage.
